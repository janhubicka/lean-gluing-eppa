# The canonical complete-overlap sparsening construction

This page traces the complete mathematical proof through the Lean modules.
Unlike the initial ordinary EPPA construction, this part starts from
a **finite EPPA-witness** with an **irreducible closed** distinguished
substructure and builds a second witness with additional geometry.

## A literal auxiliary relation

`AuxiliaryJ.lean` defines
`JSymbol`, `WithJ`, and `withJ`.
The new relation `J` is genuinely binary in the Lean language
and is made complete on the initial ambient witness exactly once.
In later refinements it is the relation contributed by the quotient
charts; the theory would not sparsify if `J` were made complete at
every stage.

A `J`-complete closed root is irreducible, and any such
overlap in a strict full-root tree lies in an actual full base block.

## Canonical charts and supported occurrences

`OrbitAtlas.lean` defines chart indices to be **actual**
closed subsets of automorphic images of the distinguished root;
there are no duplicate embedding presentations.

`CompleteChartGraph.lean` creates the complete simple graph on
these indices, with Boolean edge labels.
`ChartAtlas.lean` quotients exactly the valid triples
`(s,chi,x)` satisfying `x ∈ s`.
Two occurrences on a common point are related by an allowed
supported Boolean walk, generated by changing the coordinate
of the connecting edge.

The `CompleteRecords` and `RecordInvariance` files prove
the equivalence between supported walks and their boundary/exterior
record. Supported walks may revisit charts, and overlapping
proper charts need not all be full. The construction never
asserts that a vertex lies in a chart that does not support it.

`UnaryAtlas` and `AtlasUnaryAction` show that the whole
set-valued unary fibre is well-defined on the quotient.
The canonical projection sends each lifted output fibre
**onto** the complete old fibre.

## Affine symmetry and coherent endpoints

For a structure automorphism `h` and a Boolean edge shift
`delta`, the lifted quotient action has the form

`L(h,delta)[s,chi,x] = [h(s), h_*chi + delta, h(x)]`.

`OrbitAction` and `OrbitEndpoint` make this an actual
automorphism of the combined relational/unary quotient structure.
A chosen system of root spokes yields a uniform endpoint map
between any two actual chart images.

`ClosedPartialAut` records isomorphisms of closed induced
substructures, `IsComposition` states true composition for
matching ranges/domains, and `OrbitExtensionFamily` proves
the entire family-level extension and coherence properties.
The same original partial-map index set is used at every stage.

## The geometric dichotomy

The core of the nontrivial geometry is:

- `AtlasSeparator` proves a chart-or-closed-cut dichotomy
  for the actual quotient.
- `AtlasRootedFreeCut` and `OrbitRootedDichotomy`
  strengthen the cut case so the *projected overlap* is closed
  and has complete auxiliary `J`.
- `IrreducibleBridge`, `OrbitIrreducible`
  and `OrbitProjectionHomEmbedding` give the exact
  meaning of irreducibility and the projection's local
  embedding property.

The cut itself is **upstairs**. It cannot be pushed to the
old quotient merely because the projection is a
homomorphism-embedding. A simple diagnostic is the
edge-preserving inclusion `P4 → C4`: the extra edge
can destroy freeness downstairs.

## Actual strict-tree amalgamation

The `TreePushout*` and `TreeHomEmbedding*`
modules construct real strong pushouts and show that
homomorphism-embeddings compose.
`TreeProjectedRootAssembly` and
`TreeProjectedFreeCut` glue local target trees using
the projected **root**, while the proof of relation
locality comes from the *actual free cut upstairs*.

`TreeFullA`, `TreeJCliqueRoot`,
`TreeRootFactorization` and `TreeBaseChange`
ensure that the gluing interfaces are contained in
actual full copies of the designated irreducible root
on both sides.

## A finite numerical induction

`OrbitLocalTreeBounded.localTreeOfBoundedOldTrees`
proves the successor of the size invariant:
if all old closed tests with at most `k` vertices
map into full-root trees, then every upstairs closed
test with at most `k+1` vertices does as well.
Each genuine proper cut side has cardinality at most
`k`; its projection cannot increase that number.

`OrbitRefinedRootExact` proves the new root is
exactly isomorphic to the old root, including **all**
unary fibres and nullary relations. By
`OrbitTreeBaseChange` the successor theorem returns
trees based on the new actual root, ready for iteration.

`OrbitFiniteTower` builds the **actual** finite tower,
the root embeddings and the composite projection. In
`OrbitTowerReduct`, the correct implication
`irreducible reduct ⇒ irreducible expansion`
preserves faithfulness after forgetting `J`.
One positive refinement is needed for faithfulness,
so the final stage is `max(1,n)`.

`SparseningTheorem` assembles one `SparseningWitness`
from all these ingredients and checks ordinary and
coherent existence theorems for the same structure.
The [published statement crosswalk](Published-statements.md)
lists the exact hypotheses and conclusion fields.

## Why the local target is a homomorphism-embedding

Nothing here proves that an arbitrary local closed test
embeds *inducedly* into a tree. Even in small graph
examples, independent Boolean valuations can retain an
induced 4-cycle through every positive number of
refinements. The correct conclusion is a locally exact
homomorphism-embedding into a strict full-root tree.
This distinction is essential to the theorem.
